## What to learn from this exchange

The customer's follow-up states the requested payment, identifies previously supplied evidence, and connects the safety issue to the immediate departure. The later response records Booking.com's acknowledgment and its explanation for the delay. A stronger follow-up could also request a specific completion date and written confirmation of the refund transaction. The additional timeline mentioned by Booking.com was not present in the retrieved email correspondence and is not reconstructed here. The claimed electrical cause is the customer's account, not an independently verified technical finding.
